# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Participants in this repository are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and directly
- focus on technical substance and reproducible facts
- accept review feedback in good faith
- avoid sharing secrets or sensitive user data

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, threats, or discrimination
- personal attacks or inflammatory language
- deliberately disruptive behavior
- publishing private credentials, tokens, or sensitive local data

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
